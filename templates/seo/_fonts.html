{% load static %}{% comment %} Self-hosted fonts. The previous Google Fonts was a render-blocking stylesheet on a third -party origin: two extra connections (DNS + TCP + TLS) had to complete before the browser could even discover the font files. Serving the faces from our own origin removes both round trips, and preloading the Persian 400 face — the one that paints the LCP text on every page — starts its download in the same burst as the HTML instead of after the CSS parses. Every @font-face in fonts.css carries font-display: swap, so text paints immediately in the fallback and re-renders when the webfont lands. {% endcomment %}
